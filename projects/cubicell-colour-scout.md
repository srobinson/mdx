## Consensus Round
- A. Pole concession: **concession**. Pure #050505/#ffffff stay the default poles for theme/black/white. Chroma-bearing roles (accent) must sit on fixed rails near L 0.22 / L 0.93; uncoloured cubes never move. Without opt-in concession, face colour has zero gamut and the product is edges/field-only forever. Contrast floor 14.3:1 is still product-safe; pay once per opt-in part, not globally.
- B. First surface: **faces**. The mass and the op-art field are faces. Edges-first (Axis Ink) is a safe demo that under-sells a colour system in live judgment. Protect seams by leaving edges on pure poles in v1 (UX), not by hiding colour on the wireframe.
- C. Vocabulary: **enum**. Evidence: compactPose face colour is index into cubePartColors; edge colour is cubeEdgeStateOwner index encode; PartColorTween carries CubePartColor not hex; resolveCubePartColor is a role table. Freeform sRGB breaks wire shape, morph endpoints, and role scarcity. Extend cubePartColors with accent (optionally accent2); resolve via partColors / polarity table.
- D. Authorship vs function: **authorship**. A cube is black because the owner said so. Coord→colour (Axis Wash) erases that the same way orientation→lightness did. Functions may modulate role→colour (palette drift), never replace per-part roles as the primary substrate.
- E. Product ranking (value, not cheapness): 1 Accent Role 2 Axis Ink 3 Axis Wash 4 Enclosure Chroma 5 Facet Hue 6 Field Tint. First slice: **Accent Role**. One sentence: mark parts with one scarce accent role and restyle the whole piece with a keyframe palette so colour is authored meaning that transitions across States.
- F. Ride-along groom: **gate cubeFaceLightnessDeltaById to workbench-only** (same discipline as edgeLightnessDelta), so artifact/thumbnail/export show faithful authored poles and accents. Defer: full face→cubeEdgeStateOwner field-owner merge (index wire still works on append); tokens.css vs themeTokens single-table rewrite; removing the face ramp entirely; freeform colour paths; Enclosure/Facet procedural modules.
- Discipline changed: scout said defer #160 until a form-vs-colour decision; after the other reports I require gating the face ramp off artifact paths inside the first colour slice, because export fidelity is a precondition for judging accents, not a later cleanup.
- Discipline reject: frontend-engineer "normalized six-digit sRGB literals as value strings" as first substrate. Collides with compact index wire, PartColorTween discrete labels, and the product rule that coherence comes from scarce roles, not open pickers. Also reject pure Axis Wash as the primary surface for the same authorship reason as rejecting #160's substrate error.
