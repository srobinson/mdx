# William Engagement Scope With 20 Percent Success Fee

## Summary

This is a draft operational engagement scope. It is not legal advice. Counsel should review enforceability, required disclosures, fee structure, expense handling, conflicts, termination, and jurisdiction specific rules before anyone signs. The draft assumes Stuart provides evidence operations, monitoring, report drafting, and vendor coordination. William signs official filings. Stuart does not guarantee recovery, does not act as counsel, does not impersonate anyone, and does not handle private keys, seed phrases, credentials, or recovered funds.

## Clear disclaimers

1. No legal advice.
2. No recovery guarantee.
3. No hacking, phishing, malware, credential use, unauthorized access, or direct scammer contact.
4. No impersonation of William, counsel, law enforcement, exchanges, services, vendors, or any other person or entity.
5. No private key handling, seed phrase handling, wallet file handling, hardware wallet PIN handling, two factor code handling, or credential handling.
6. Counsel should review enforceability before signature.

### Scope of work

Stuart may provide the following operations support:

1. Evidence organization, evidence index creation, file hash tracking, and source file preservation support.
2. Transaction inventory preparation using client supplied records and public blockchain data.
3. Public blockchain monitoring and movement alert notes.
4. Draft report language and packet preparation for IC3, local police, FBI field office, FTC, Chainabuse, FixedFloat, Binance or Binance.US support, analytics vendors, and counsel.
5. Analytics vendor quote collection and scope coordination.
6. Status tracking, deadline tracking, report number tracking, ticket number tracking, and client decision logs.
7. Lawful handoff support to counsel, law enforcement, services, exchanges, and analytics vendors.

### Out of scope

Stuart will not provide:

1. Legal advice, legal conclusions, legal threats, demand letters with legal effect, litigation strategy, subpoena drafting, or court filings.
2. Law enforcement action, exchange freeze demands, account record requests, or requests for protected customer data outside lawful channels.
3. Recovery guarantees or claims that private recovery is likely.
4. Hacking, phishing, malware, credential use, unauthorized access, social engineering, dark market contact, or direct scammer engagement.
5. Impersonation of William, counsel, law enforcement, exchanges, services, vendors, or any third party.
6. Private key, seed phrase, hardware wallet PIN, wallet file, two factor code, password, or credential handling.
7. Custody of recovered cryptocurrency or fiat funds.
8. Public disclosure of sensitive case details without William's written approval and counsel review if needed.

### Client responsibilities

William is responsible for:

1. Confirming identity, ownership, domicile, and correct incident date range.
2. Providing source evidence and prior report records.
3. Reviewing and approving draft narratives, transaction tables, and packets.
4. Signing and submitting official filings and victim statements unless counsel directs another lawful process.
5. Approving vendor expenses in writing before costs are incurred.
6. Deciding whether to engage counsel.
7. Keeping private keys, seed phrases, PINs, credentials, and two factor codes away from Stuart.

### Success fee

Subject to counsel review, William agrees to pay Stuart **20 percent of net recovered value** only if recovered funds are actually returned to William or to a counsel controlled account for William's benefit.

Recommended definition:

> Success fee means 20 percent of net cryptocurrency or fiat value actually returned to William's possession or to a counsel controlled account for William's benefit, after the funds are cleared, accessible, and no longer subject to immediate reversal or competing claimant hold.

### Fee exclusions

No success fee is owed for:

1. Funds merely identified, traced, watched, reported, or frozen.
2. Funds returned entirely because of prior law enforcement, prior counsel, prior exchange action, insurance, tax deductions, goodwill credits, or unrelated account recoveries, unless counsel approves a specific written inclusion.
3. Expense reimbursements.
4. Amounts William pays to vendors, counsel, law enforcement related costs, notaries, courts, or filing services.
5. Any recovery that is still subject to immediate reversal, clawback, competing claimant hold, sanctions concern, or unresolved legal restriction.

### Expenses

1. No recovery deposit is required.
2. No upfront fee may be described as necessary to unlock funds.
3. Analytics, counsel, filing, notarization, records, travel, or vendor costs require William's prior written approval.
4. William should pay vendors directly when practical.
5. If Stuart advances an approved cost, reimbursement requires a receipt and must stay within the written cap.
6. Stuart receives no undisclosed markup on vendor costs.

### Conflicts, referrals, and vendors

1. Stuart must disclose any referral fees, affiliate relationships, vendor relationships, prior work with analytics firms, or other potential conflicts.
2. No fee sharing with counsel unless counsel confirms in writing that the arrangement is permitted.
3. Counsel's professional judgment remains independent.
4. William may reject any vendor recommendation.
5. Vendor scope should be narrow: validate or reject the Wasabi demix candidate, live BTC linkage, and probable Binance branch.

### Private key and custody boundary

1. Stuart will not ask for or handle private keys, seed phrases, wallet files, hardware wallet PINs, two factor codes, exchange passwords, or recovery phrases.
2. William should not send those materials to Stuart.
3. Any recovered funds should return to William's account or a counsel controlled account.
4. Stuart should not hold recovered funds, sign transactions, operate William's wallets, or receive custody unless counsel creates a narrow written structure. Recommended default is no custody.

### Termination

1. Either party may terminate in writing.
2. On termination, Stuart stops new work and external outreach.
3. Stuart returns or destroys client data as William directs, subject to counsel or law enforcement preservation needs.
4. Approved expenses incurred before termination remain reimbursable.
5. The success fee survives only for recoveries causally tied to Stuart's work and only during the written tail period approved by counsel.
6. No expense incurred after termination is reimbursable unless William approves it in writing.

### No guarantee and recovery scam protection

Stuart does not guarantee recovery. William should reject any person or company that promises recovery, demands an upfront unlock fee, asks for seed phrases or private keys, asks for credentials, or insists on payment before returning funds.

### Counsel review recommendation

Counsel should review this draft before signature, especially:

1. Whether a 20 percent success fee for operations support is enforceable in William's jurisdiction.
2. Whether additional consumer protection, private investigator, unauthorized practice, fee sharing, or referral rules apply.
3. Whether the success fee definition, exclusions, tail period, and expense terms are adequate.
4. Whether communications should route through counsel for privilege or work product reasons.
5. Whether any service or exchange outreach should be sent by counsel or law enforcement.
