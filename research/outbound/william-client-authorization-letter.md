# William Client Authorization Letter

**Draft date:** 2026-05-25  
**For counsel review if counsel is engaged.**

## Summary

This draft lets William authorize Stuart to help with evidence organization, draft reports, analytics vendor intake, service packets, and monitoring. William remains responsible for signing official filings, approving victim statements, and deciding whether to engage counsel. Stuart does not provide legal advice, does not guarantee recovery, does not impersonate William, and does not handle private keys or seed phrases.

## Clear disclaimers

1. No legal advice.
2. No recovery guarantee.
3. No hacking, phishing, malware, credential use, unauthorized access, or direct scammer contact.
4. No impersonation of William, counsel, law enforcement, exchanges, services, vendors, or any other person or entity.
5. No private key handling, seed phrase handling, wallet file handling, hardware wallet PIN handling, two factor code handling, or credential handling.

## Draft Letter

To whom it may concern,

I, **William [full legal name]**, authorize **Stuart [full legal name or entity]** to assist me with operational support for my reported cryptocurrency theft matter.

### Authorized support

I authorize Stuart to:

1. Organize evidence that I provide, including CSV files, screenshots, transaction hashes, explorer links, emails, chats, ticket numbers, report numbers, and related records.
2. Create evidence indexes, transaction inventories, file hash manifests, case summaries, status logs, and attachment lists.
3. Draft report language and service packets for my review, including drafts for IC3, local police, FBI field office, FTC, Chainabuse, FixedFloat, Binance or Binance.US support, analytics vendors, and counsel.
4. Contact blockchain analytics vendors for quotes, deliverable descriptions, timing, conflict checks, and scope discussions.
5. Contact service support or victim support channels for intake, preservation routing, and ticket creation, when I am copied unless we agree otherwise in writing.
6. Monitor public blockchain addresses and preserve public data, including raw API responses, screenshots, txids, timestamps, inputs, outputs, and explorer links.
7. Prepare operational notes, missing information requests, and supplemental update drafts if new transactions or service responses appear.

### Authority limits

This authorization does not allow Stuart to:

1. Provide legal advice or act as my lawyer.
2. Promise or guarantee recovery.
3. Sign, file, or submit official victim statements, complaints, affidavits, declarations, court papers, or law enforcement reports in my name, unless counsel approves a specific process in writing.
4. Impersonate me, counsel, law enforcement, an exchange, a service, a vendor, or any other person or entity.
5. Hack, phish, use malware, guess credentials, use unauthorized access, contact scammers directly, or interact with suspect addresses.
6. Handle, request, store, transmit, or view my private keys, seed phrases, hardware wallet PINs, wallet files, two factor codes, or credentials.
7. Take custody of recovered cryptocurrency or fiat funds.
8. Publicly post sensitive case details unless I approve the exact text in writing and counsel reviews it if needed.
9. Spend money, retain vendors, or commit me to expenses without my prior written approval.

### Official filings and victim statements

I understand that official reports and victim statements should be submitted by me or by counsel. Stuart may prepare drafts, attachments, transaction tables, and summaries for my review. I will review, approve, sign, and submit official filings unless counsel directs another lawful process.

### External communication statement

When contacting vendors, services, or support channels under this authorization, Stuart may say:

> I am assisting William with evidence organization and operations support under written authorization. William is copied on this communication. Please direct any official filing, identity verification, legal process, victim signature, account access, or privileged legal request to William or his counsel.

### Evidence and confidentiality

Stuart may store and organize materials I provide for this matter. Stuart should preserve originals when possible, avoid altering source evidence, and keep a record of file names, collection dates, hashes, and notes. Stuart should not share sensitive case materials outside the work described above without my approval or counsel direction.

### Duration and revocation

This authorization begins on the date I sign it and remains effective until I revoke it in writing. I may revoke it at any time. After revocation, Stuart should stop external outreach, return or destroy my materials as I direct, and preserve only records that counsel or law enforcement instructs should be retained.

### No legal advice and no guarantee

I understand that Stuart is not providing legal advice and cannot guarantee recovery. Recovery depends on validated evidence, service cooperation, law enforcement or court process, and facts not yet confirmed.
