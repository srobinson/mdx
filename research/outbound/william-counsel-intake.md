### Proposed operational role for Stuart

Stuart would provide client operations support only:

1. Organize raw evidence without altering originals.
2. Build transaction inventory and evidence index files.
3. Draft IC3, local police, FBI field office, FTC, Chainabuse, FixedFloat, Binance, analytics vendor, and counsel packet language for William or counsel to review.
4. Monitor public blockchain addresses and preserve raw API responses, screenshots, txids, timestamps, inputs, outputs, and explorer links.
5. Contact analytics vendors for quotes and scope, with William copied unless otherwise approved in writing.
6. Maintain status logs, report numbers, ticket numbers, vendor quotes, expense approvals, and next actions.

### Operating boundaries

1. No legal advice.
2. No recovery guarantee.
3. No hacking, phishing, malware, credential use, unauthorized access, dark market contact, or direct scammer engagement.
4. No impersonation of William, counsel, law enforcement, exchanges, or analytics vendors.
5. No private key, seed phrase, hardware wallet PIN, wallet file, two factor code, or recovered asset custody.
6. No public disclosure of sensitive case details unless William approves the exact text and counsel advises if needed.
7. No overclaiming low confidence Wasabi or Binance attribution.
8. No vendor expense without prior written approval.

### Questions for counsel

1. Which jurisdiction's law governs the engagement, given William's domicile and any relevant business or asset facts?
2. Can Stuart provide evidence organization, monitoring, report drafting, vendor intake, and operations support under a written client authorization without crossing into legal services?
3. Should Stuart's work be routed through counsel to support privilege or work product protection? If yes, what engagement structure and communication protocol should be used?
4. Is the proposed 20 percent upside enforceable for a not legal service provider in the relevant jurisdiction? If enforceable, what disclosures, limits, tail period, valuation method, and expense terms are required?
5. Would any part of the arrangement implicate fee sharing, referral fee, private investigator licensing, debt collection, asset recovery, consumer protection, or unauthorized practice rules?
6. Who should send preservation letters or legal process to FixedFloat, Binance, Binance.US, other exchanges, or analytics vendors?
7. Which reports should William file personally, and which can be prepared by Stuart for William's signature or counsel submission?
8. Should any Chainabuse report remain private until counsel approves public visibility?
9. What evidence retention, hashing, privacy, and data transfer protocol does counsel want Stuart to follow?
10. If the live BTC moves, what immediate escalation channel should Stuart use, and who is authorized to contact law enforcement, counsel, exchanges, or analytics vendors?
11. Should recovered funds ever pass through Stuart? Recommended default is no. If there is a recovery, funds should return to William or a counsel controlled account.
12. Are there insurance, tax, insolvency, probate, marital, business ownership, investor, or reporting constraints that change the plan?
