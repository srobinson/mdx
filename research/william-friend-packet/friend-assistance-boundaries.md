## Summary

The clean boundary is practical friend assistance. Stuart organizes evidence, prepares summaries, drafts copy and paste messages, and tracks facts. William reviews, sends, signs, files, verifies identity, speaks to law enforcement or vendors, and decides whether to hire counsel or paid analytics.

Lead fact for William: a live BTC watchlist address remains the best current operational lead. ## Details

### What Stuart can do

1. Organize William's source files without changing originals.
2. Build and maintain the transaction inventory, evidence index, and attachment checklist.
3. Draft IC3, police, FBI, Chainabuse, FixedFloat, Binance, and paid analytics messages for William to review.
4. Prepare short instructions that tell William exactly what to copy, paste, fill, attach, and send.
5. Monitor public blockchain activity for the live BTC address and notify William if it moves.
6. Keep language conservative: reported theft, watchlist lead, preservation request, validation needed.
7. Help William compare vendor quotes or counsel options, without making promises about recovery.

### What William does himself

1. Confirms all facts, dates, amounts, wallet ownership details, and prior report numbers.
2. Sends every external message under his own name.
3. Signs and files IC3, local police, FTC, Chainabuse, exchange, service, insurance, or counsel materials.
4. Completes identity checks and account verification.
5. Decides whether to retain counsel or pay an analytics vendor.
6. Controls all private keys, seed phrases, exchange credentials, two factor codes, wallet files, and recovered funds.
7. Saves receipts, complaint numbers, ticket numbers, names, timestamps, and copies of every submission.

### Hard boundaries

1. No seed phrases, private keys, wallet files, hardware wallet PINs, two factor codes, exchange passwords, or recovery phrases go to Stuart.
2. No hacking, phishing, credential guessing, malware, social engineering, dark market contact, or direct scammer contact.
3. No custody. Recovered assets, if any, go only to William or a counsel controlled account.
4. No guarantees. Anyone promising recovery, demanding an unlock fee, or asking for secrets should be treated as a likely recovery scam.
5. No impersonation. William sends as William. Stuart's role is drafting and organization.
6. No public posting of sensitive identifiers until William approves the exact content and considers counsel input.
7. No claim that the live BTC is proven stolen funds unless paid analytics, law enforcement, or service records validate the low confidence path.
