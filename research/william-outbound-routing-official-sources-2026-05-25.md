# Executive Summary

Official source checks support conservative outbound routing for William's case. FixedFloat publishes direct crime victim support and law enforcement addresses, Binance.com routes law enforcement through its official LERS page and Kodex, Binance.US separates itself from Binance.com records, Chainabuse supports private reports, TRM accepts contact inquiries, and Chainalysis warns that it does not assist individual victims directly.

# Detailed Findings

## FixedFloat routing

FixedFloat's support page lists `help@fixedfloat.com` for crime victim support, `compliance@fixedfloat.com` for suspended order support, and `legal@fixedfloat.com` for law enforcement requests. The FixedFloat draft therefore uses victim support first, copies compliance, and asks for the law enforcement route without requesting protected customer records.

Source: https://ff.io/en/support

## Binance routing

Binance.com publishes a Government Law Enforcement Request System page for government and law enforcement agencies. The page directs global law enforcement agencies to the Kodex Binance portal, states that the portal is for agency use, and says exigent requests should be marked as exigent under legal process type.

Source: https://www.binance.com/en/support/law-enforcement

Binance.com also publishes Binance Verify and a support chat link. The victim support draft uses these only for ticketing and authenticity checks, not for records or freeze demands.

Sources:

1. https://www.binance.com/en/chat?sourceEntry=4
2. https://www.binance.com/en/official-verification

## Binance.US routing boundary

Binance.US publishes a separate request form and a law enforcement guide. The guide states victims should contact support or IC3, and that BAM Trading and Binance Holdings Limited are separate legal companies. It also states BAM Trading cannot respond to records requests addressed to Binance. Formal records and freeze requests are law enforcement matters.

Sources:

1. https://support.binance.us/hc/en-us/requests/new
2. https://support.binance.us/en/articles/9842980-binance-us-law-enforcement-guide

## Paid analytics routing

TRM Labs publishes a general contact page with `contact@trmlabs.com` and markets court defensible investigation tooling. This supports using TRM or a similarly reputable vendor for a validation only inquiry.

Sources:

1. https://www.trmlabs.com/contact-us
2. https://www.trmlabs.com/blockchain-intelligence-platform/forensics

Chainalysis warns that it does not provide individual assistance with recovering funds or resolving fraud cases. The paid analytics inquiry therefore avoids implying Chainalysis will help an individual victim directly and asks vendors to state the correct intake route.

Source: https://www.chainalysis.com/scams/

## Chainabuse private reporting

Chainabuse FAQ says sensitive cases can be reported privately and that private report information is visible to law enforcement partners rather than published publicly. Chainabuse docs identify required report inputs and optional evidence, loss, indicators, and contact consent fields. This supports private first report notes and public reporting only with William's approval.

Sources:

1. https://chainabuse.com/faq/GENERAL
2. https://docs.chainabuse.com/docs/post-reports-parameters
