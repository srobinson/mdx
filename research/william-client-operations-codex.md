# Executive Summary

William's case is actionable as a preservation, reporting, monitoring, and lawful escalation matter. Stuart can organize evidence, monitor the live on chain lead, prepare report packets, and coordinate lawful handoffs, while William, law enforcement, counsel, exchanges, and paid analytics providers must handle the parts that require legal authority, account ownership, subpoenas, freezes, or forensic validation.

The operating posture should be strict: no recovery guarantees, no private key handling, no hacking, no impersonation, no direct scammer engagement, and no request for confidential exchange data outside lawful process. A 20 percent contingency structure can be considered only with a written scope, clear success definition, client expense controls, conflict disclosures, and counsel review.

## Recommended Engagement Structure for a 20 Percent Contingency Upside

This is operational guidance, not legal advice. Counsel should review before signature.

### Written scope

Scope should say Stuart provides non legal client operations support:

1. Evidence organization.
2. Transaction inventory.
3. Public blockchain monitoring.
4. Report draft preparation.
5. Vendor coordination.
6. Status tracking.
7. Lawful handoff support.

Scope should exclude:

1. Legal advice.
2. Law enforcement action.
3. Exchange subpoenas or freeze demands.
4. Litigation.
5. Asset custody.
6. Private key handling.
7. Direct scammer engagement.
8. Guaranteed recovery.

### Success fee definition

Use a narrow definition:

```text
Success fee means 20 percent of net cryptocurrency or fiat value actually returned to William's possession or to a counsel controlled account for William's benefit, after the funds are cleared, accessible, and no longer subject to immediate reversal or competing claimant hold.
```

Define exclusions:

1. No fee for funds merely located.
2. No fee for funds merely frozen.
3. No fee for tax deductions, insurance proceeds, goodwill credits, or unrelated account recoveries unless expressly included.
4. No fee for recovery caused entirely by prior law enforcement, prior counsel, or prior exchange action unless the agreement defines a tail and counsel approves it.
5. No fee on money William pays as expenses.

Define valuation:

1. For crypto returned as crypto, use the exchange rate at the time William gains control, from a named price source.
2. For fiat returned, use actual net fiat received.
3. For mixed recoveries, attach a calculation schedule.

### Expenses

1. No recovery deposit.
2. No upfront fee represented as required to unlock funds.
3. Vendor costs require written approval before commitment.
4. William should pay vendors directly where practical.
5. If reimbursement is needed, require receipts and a written cap.
6. Counsel should review any escrow or trust structure.

### Conflicts and referrals

1. Disclose any referral fees, vendor relationships, affiliate relationships, or prior work with analytics firms.
2. No undisclosed markup on paid analytics.
3. No fee sharing with counsel unless counsel confirms it is permitted.
4. If counsel is engaged, counsel's professional judgment must stay independent.

### Termination

1. Either party may terminate in writing.
2. Stuart returns or destroys client data as directed, subject to evidence preservation needs.
3. The success fee survives only for recoveries causally tied to Stuart's work and only for a defined tail period approved by counsel.
4. Expenses incurred without written approval are not reimbursable.

### Counsel review triggers

Counsel should review before:

1. Signing the contingency agreement.
2. Sending legal preservation letters.
3. Contacting exchanges beyond customer support or victim support.
4. Making accusations of attribution.
5. Filing claims in any forfeiture or restitution process.
6. Receiving recovered funds.
7. Publishing case details.
